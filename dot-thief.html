Dot Thief

You are the night guard, and you win a level by catching the thief before it steals every coin in the vault. Each catch scores 1,000 points times the level, plus 10 points for every coin still on the floor.

Ready to play?

Steer the guard with the arrow keys or WASD, and catch the thief before it collects every coin. If the thief sets off a smoke bomb, you are blinded for a few seconds and it can knock you out, so keep your distance until the smoke clears.

Steer the guard with the arrow keys or WASD, press Space or P to pause, and press Enter to start. Press Esc (or Alt+Shift+H) to go back to all games.
