Ready to play?

Watch closely as some tiles light up. When they go dark, pick every tile that was lit. Each wrong pick costs you a life.

Use the arrow keys to move around the board, and press Space or Enter to pick a tile. You can also press Enter to start a game. Press Esc (or Alt+Shift+H) to go back to all games.
